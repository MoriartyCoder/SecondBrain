## USC thermal drone dataset
USC thermal drone dataset. It contains 10 thermal video clips shot at the USC campus and sample images are demonstrated in Fig. 7. All of them were shot with the same drone model as that for USC drone dataset. Each video clip is approximately one minute and the frame resolution is 1920 x 1080. The frame rate is 30 frames per second.
