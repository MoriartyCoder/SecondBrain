

### Why use 3x3 kernel?
1. Less parameter need to be trained
2. Less FLOPs opertations
3. Two 3x3 conv layers are needed to replace a 5x5 conv layer in terms of receptive field. But due to the additional non-linearity layer 3x3 is preferred
